# C — Minimal low-profile

**Intent:** Disappear on the dog. Lowest crown, flush faces, fasteners recessed
so nothing catches brush or harness webbing. No seams, no facets, no badges.

## Form

- Cap height **18 mm** (floor 5 + cavity 13) — a hair under the slim production
  19 mm so the silhouette reads flatter under the panel.
- Corner radius R≈3 everywhere; micro 0.8 mm top break only (print edge quality,
  not decoration).
- Deep Ø8 counterbores on lid / tub screws so button heads sit below the outer
  face (cap with a cover plug or paint later if desired).
- Counterweight tub trimmed to 28 mm tall for a shorter throat block.

## Hardware envelope

Columns and clamps unchanged. Cap floor 5 mm still clears M3×4 clamp inserts
with ≥1 mm keep-out to the cavity. Pod cavity remains **82×58×21.5**.

## Print

Simplest plate: flat bed face, almost no overhang. Hidden cbore floors are
horizontal — print as-is (acceptable) or add a 0.2 mm sacrificial skin and
drill post-print if a perfect bridge is required.

## Trade-offs

- Least “designed” at a glance — beauty is in the absence of clutter.
- Recessed heads need plugs or paint if water-shedding on the cap top matters.
- Shorter CW tub leaves less foam pack above the steel stack.
