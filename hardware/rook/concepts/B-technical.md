# B — Clean technical

**Intent:** Instrument-grade housing — planar faces, a deliberate 45° chamfer on
the crown, and **one** accent seam that wraps the waist so the eye has a single
datum instead of a scatter of facets.

## Form

- Near-rectilinear footprint with a tight corner radius (R≈2) so the outline
  stays crisp on the strap.
- Top edges chamfered (~3–3.5 mm, 1-segment bevel → true 45°).
- Accent seam: 1.2 mm deep × ~1.6 mm wall groove at ~40–45% height — painted,
  contrasting filament, or left as a shadow line.
- Same port / cable cutouts on the pod as production.

## Hardware envelope

Identical column / clamp / cavity envelope to Node V1. Seam sits in the outer
wall only and does not thin past 1 mm keep-out to the cavity.

## Print

Flat on bed, chamfers self-supporting. Seam overhang is shallow (horizontal
floor of the groove) — acceptable on 0.16–0.20 mm PLA; for ASA prefer the
groove facing up (print as today) or fill with a contrasting co-extrusion later.

## Trade-offs

- Chamfer + seam add visual “product” cues but also a dust ledge.
- Less friendly to hand-polishing than concept A; edges show layer lines.
- Lid / O-ring not in this shell study.
